# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 20 October 2026_

This site (learnwith.mahidhardurga.in) is a static website of educational articles.

## Information we collect

The site has no user accounts and does not ask you for personal information. Search runs entirely in your browser against a pre-built index. The outside services the site uses are the reader counter and Google AdSense advertising, both described below.

Like any web host, GitHub Pages may log technical data such as IP addresses and user agents. See the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Reader counter

The top bar of every page shows a reader count from a free counter service (abacus.jasoncameron.dev). Your browser asks it to add one the first time you visit, and it sees your IP address like any website you load. No cookies are used for this.

## Advertising (Google AdSense)

This site uses Google AdSense to show advertising, so that the articles can stay free.

- Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this site and other websites.
- Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and other sites on the internet.
- Where the law requires it (for example for visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), a consent message is shown so that you can accept or refuse the use of cookies and similar technologies for advertising. You can change your choice later from the same message.
- You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com), or opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at [www.aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info).
- To learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services, see [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

This site does not sell your personal information.

## Cookies and local storage

The site stores your light/dark theme choice in your browser's local storage, along with a small flag that remembers you were already counted so that refreshing does not raise the reader count. Neither is sent to any server by this site. Google and its advertising partners may set their own cookies as described above.

## Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated when the site changes how it uses cookies, advertising or analytics. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
