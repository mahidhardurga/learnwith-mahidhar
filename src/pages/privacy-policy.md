# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 17 October 2026_

This site (learnwith.mahidhardurga.in) is a static website of educational articles.

## Information we collect

At this time the site does not run analytics, advertising or tracking scripts, has no user accounts and does not collect personal information. Search runs entirely in your browser against a pre-built index. The only outside request the site makes is the reader counter described below.

Like any web host, GitHub Pages may log technical data such as IP addresses and user agents. See the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Reader counter

The top bar of every page shows a reader count from a free counter service (abacus.jasoncameron.dev). Your browser asks it to add one the first time you visit, and it sees your IP address like any website you load. No cookies are used for this.

## Cookies and local storage

The site stores your light/dark theme choice in your browser's local storage, along with a small flag that remembers you were already counted so that refreshing does not raise the reader count. Neither is sent to any server.

## Future changes

This policy will be updated before any analytics, advertising or affiliate features are added, including a description of the cookies involved and how to opt out.
